Run: `UXR-20260810-0141`

## Coverage result

All **29 implemented App Router pages** received an accepted baseline capture at all six required viewports: **174/174 canonical route/viewport cells**. The non-implemented legacy probe `/customers` was also captured at all six viewports and consistently returned the Next.js 404; it is recorded separately so it does not inflate the 29-route inventory.

This is visual route coverage, not proof that every workflow, permission, data state, API contract, keyboard path, or assistive-technology behavior passed. “Deep” means an interaction beyond the stable route baseline was exercised; “partial” and “blocked/skipped” preserve the limits in the approved module reports.

Cell values are accepted screenshot identifiers in the route's evidence folder. `✓` means the stable route baseline was captured and visually inspected.

## Coverage boundaries

- Baseline completeness: **29 routes × 6 viewports = 174/174 accepted cells**.
- Extra legacy probe: **6/6 `/customers` 404 captures**.
- Deep coverage is intentionally uneven. Required deep interaction widths were met for Task 1 disclosures and Task 2 dynamic table/status routes; other modules document partial, blocked, or safely skipped states above.
- Only the seeded admin session was broadly available. Lower-privilege, cross-outlet, 401/403, expired-token, offline, slow-network, clean-session, and physical-device matrices remain incomplete.
- Screenshot/DOM observations support the recorded accessibility risks. They do not constitute full keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, zoom, reduced-motion, high-contrast, or WCAG conformance testing.
- Matrix acceptance means the screenshot was stable and inspected; it does not mean the route passed its functional or design review.
