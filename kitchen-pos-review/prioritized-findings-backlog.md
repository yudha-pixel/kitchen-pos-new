# Prioritized cross-module findings backlog

Run: `UXR-20260810-0141`

## Classification and severity

- **Confirmed defect**: visible/runtime behavior was reproduced, with source or data corroboration where stated.
- **Architecture risk**: static design/ownership/enforcement creates material exposure, but the full runtime consequence was not proven across every role or environment.
- **Optional design recommendation**: target-state improvement that should not displace trust, transaction, accessibility, or responsive repairs.
- **P0**: none confirmed. The audit did not demonstrate catastrophic irreversible loss, protected-data compromise, or a system-wide outage.
- **P1**: release-blocking primary journey, transaction/configuration truth, authorization, or serious operability problem.
- **P2**: important defect/risk with a bounded workaround or narrower surface.
- **P3**: bounded quality/evolution item.

Approval of the six module reports means their evidence and wording passed independent review. It does **not** mean the product passed release acceptance.

## Delivery sequence

The accepted sequence is maintained in [phased-roadmap.md](./phased-roadmap.md):

1. **Phase 0:** contain auth, transaction, write, calculation, camera, and false-success failures; establish shared accessible errors/dialogs and runtime config.
2. **Phase 1:** capability enforcement, `/apps`, module shells, responsive shell, settings precedence, snapshots, semantic themes.
3. **Phase 2:** rehabilitate each module workflow and complete state/accessibility evidence.
4. **Phase 3:** trusted internal plugin architecture only after the shared gates pass.
5. **Phase 4:** optional product evolution from validated demand.
